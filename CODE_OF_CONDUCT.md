# Code of conduct

This is a small project. The rules are short and they are meant literally.

**Expected of everyone here**

- Assume the other person is acting in good faith, and be specific rather than sharp when you disagree.
- Critique the code, the evidence or the argument — never the person.
- Remember that contributors and reporters are volunteers spending their own time, and that not
  everyone shares your background, toolchain, language or level of experience.
- When you are wrong, say so and move on. It costs nothing here.

**Not acceptable, anywhere in this project**

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory or sexualised remarks, deliberate intimidation,
sustained disruption, publishing someone's private information, or persistently pushing past a clear
"no".

**Scope**

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and any other space that is part
of this project, and to conduct elsewhere that has a direct effect on it.

**If something goes wrong**

Report it privately to the maintainers using GitHub's private reporting on this repository
(Security → Report a vulnerability works for conduct reports too, and is the most private channel
available here). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainers may edit or remove content, and
may block accounts, when that is what it takes to keep the project a reasonable place to be.
