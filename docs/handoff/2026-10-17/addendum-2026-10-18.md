## 5. Ordra runtime defects (Ordra, not Cernum)

Both were found on the Mac mini's interim Ordra runtime. Full detail is in [`ordra-runtime-defects.md`](ordra-runtime-defects.md).

1. **A campaign moved to a new goal version cannot plan a launchable pass.**
   - After `repin-campaign`, `plan-pass` checks the campaign's derived (old-version) criteria, but work-order assembly requires the pinned (new-version) criteria.
   - Workaround: cancel the campaign and derive a continuation under the new version.
2. **A malformed verification command passes planning and then blocks every lane without raising a decision.**
   - A shell-quoted command was accepted by `plan-pass` and refused only at verification (VF-9).
   - The withheld attempt consumed each cycle's single action for about 1 h 50 min, and no escalation was raised.
   - Recovery: `abandon-local-attempt`, then `retire-pass`.
