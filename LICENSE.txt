Model Lab

Model Lab is a local benchmarking laboratory for language models that run on this computer
through Ollama. It sends fixed, synthetic prompts to the models you choose, judges the answers with
fixed rules, and keeps every result on this computer as permanent evidence.

Model Lab does not collect personal data, does not send anything to the internet, and does not
install language models unless you explicitly ask it to download one. Benchmark results are evidence
for your own decisions; Model Lab changes nothing about how any model is used anywhere.

Model Lab is provided as is, without warranty of any kind. Use it at your own risk.

Uninstalling Model Lab removes the application but keeps your evidence store (under your user
application data folder) so your history is never silently deleted.
